# Child safety and ethical design (applies everywhere)

Based on UK Age Appropriate Design Code std. 13, COPPA (amended 2025), Radesky et al. 2022.
- No countdown pressure, no loot boxes, no purchases, no ads, no streak-loss messages.
- The mascot or characters never guilt the child for being away.
- Weekly goal (5 of 7) with built-in rest days instead of a fragile daily streak.
- Coins reward competence, never time played or logins (Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999).
- Leaderboards: none global. Future leagues must be small, weekly, same age, top-10 plus own rank.
- Data: nickname, grade and grammatical gender only. No names, emails, location or photos.
- The values village never scores or ranks children.
